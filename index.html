Mental Health & Wellness

A sanctuary for the 'strong' to be soft, and for your true essence to finally be seen.

Essence of You is a therapy practice for individuals and couples seeking authenticity, balance, peace, and meaningful change. We support those who are ready to move beyond anxiety, depression, burnout, and disconnection into a life that feels more authentic and aligned. Therapy should feel like that long-awaited exhale you’ve been denying yourself, and the one place where you don’t have to be strong or perform. It should be a safe space to lay down your armor, speak your truth, and do the deeper work of creating the life and mindset you desire. As an affirming, culturally responsive practice, clients of all backgrounds and relationship structures are welcome, including BIPOC, LGBTQIA+ individuals, and those in polyamorous or consensual non-monogamous relationships. We don't just solve problems here; we curate the life and love you deserve where your identity is honored, not just "included".

About Us

Dr. Alisha Diggs, LPC-S

As a therapist, a wife, a mother, an educator, and someone who understands, personally and professionally, what it means to carry the weight of "doing it all," while still having to keep showing up for others everyday, I see you, because I am you. With over 15 years of experience, I’ve worked in higher education and clinical settings supporting people through stress, depression, trauma, life transitions, and relationship challenges. My PhD in Marriage and Family Therapy focused on systemic therapy, which means I look beyond surface-level issues to help you understand and shift the patterns shaping your life and relationships. I work especially well with college students, high-achieving or over-functioning clients navigating burnout, LGBTQ+ clients, and with couples who desire to feel connected again, whether in traditional or non-traditional relationships. My style is warm, direct, and affirming, with a focus on honesty, emotional safety, and real change. Welcome to a space designed for your rest, your healing, and your highest self.

Our Mission

A Safe, Inclusive & Empowering Environment

Therapy is a sacred space where the "strong" persona can finally rest and the mask can come off. My approach blends traditional insight with wellness practices that honor your mental, emotional, and spiritual well-being. We’ll look back at the stories that shaped you, learn how they influence your present, and work on building the future you deserve. By the end of our work together, our mission is to move you from "just getting through the day" to living with a deep sense of self-awareness, resilience, authenticity, and the quiet confidence of someone who is finally at home in their own skin.
